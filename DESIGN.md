# Rinse Forge design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile car detailing for Houston drivers who want daily cabin buildup, exterior road film, wheels, glass, and painted surfaces handled as clear work zones. The page's single job is to move a driver toward calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Iron — `#202426`
- Hot steel — `#C95636`
- Quench — `#65AEB4`
- Vapor — `#E8EEEC`
- Clean white — `#FAFBF8`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

Use a bright workshop ledger that moves from visible condition to working zones and a whole-vehicle review.

## Signature

One forged-metal edge becomes a clean waterline through the hero and process sequence.

## Critique

The initial clone still read as soft, generic luxury detailing. The revised identity uses iron, quench blue, and one hot-steel accent; the bold gesture now describes an actual wash-and-review path instead of a decorative wheel arc.
